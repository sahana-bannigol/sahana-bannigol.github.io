// 01. about

Who I Am

How It Started

My journey into blockchain didn't begin with Bitcoin charts or a crypto Twitter rabbit hole. It started in a seminar room during my third year of engineering, where I had to present a research paper. The paper I chose was on an e-voting system built on blockchain. Until then, I hadn't touched a wallet or heard of a smart contract. But reading it, something clicked — the idea of a transparent, tamper-proof system where no single authority holds the truth felt genuinely radical. That seminar changed my direction entirely.

Skin in the Game

After that, I had to understand it from the inside. I started tracking how cryptocurrency moved — reading white papers, watching on-chain activity, making sense of market cycles. I signed up on WazirX, bought Dogecoin, and lost money. Genuinely. But losing that money was the best early education I could have got — it made me understand volatility, sentiment, and the very real gap between hype and utility. The thrill wasn't the gains; it was the mechanics: a system running without anyone being in charge. I was hooked.

From Curiosity to Code

That curiosity became a career. I learned Solidity, built smart contracts, and pursued an MSc in FinTech at the University of Exeter, graduating with Distinction. My thesis — G-LOC — collapsed B2B trade-finance settlement from 10–14 days to under 24 hours using Ethereum smart contracts. Before that, 3 years as a backend engineer at MapleLabs gave me deep fluency in Go, Kafka, Kubernetes, and AWS — the infrastructure that high-throughput on-chain systems sit on top of. Now at Nexa, an L1 blockchain, I'm building the full stack: REST APIs, NFT minting, Token Groups, and on-chain database indexing.

What I'm Looking For

I want to be part of teams that are building things that matter — solving problems that haven't been cracked yet. The problems I keep coming back to are cross-border payments (why does moving money across borders in 2026 still take days and lose value to intermediaries?) and data provenance (how do we create auditable, trustless trails for assets, documents, and compliance records at scale?). I want to grow fast, be around engineers who push me, and contribute to real-world FinTech infrastructure that reaches people who actually need it.
